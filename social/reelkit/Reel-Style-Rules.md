# Ohana Reel Style Rules

Adapted from the 5-Pass Reel Edit Kit (@zeeuiux) to the Ohana brand system. `render.py` follows these on every reel.

## Canvas
- 1080x1920, 30 fps, H.264, AAC 48 kHz.
- Safe area: nothing important in the top 220 px, the bottom 380 px or the right 140 px (Instagram UI).
  Text and the CTA live inside x 140–940, y 220–1540.

## Type
- Headlines: Anton, all caps, max 3 lines, auto-shrunk to fit 800 px.
- One key word per headline sits on the gold highlight bar. Never two.
- Sublines: DM Serif Display italic, cream with a thin black outline.
- Max 2 fonts on screen.

## Color (Ohana brand system)
- Forest green `#1A3D2B` (brand bar, end card).
- Gold `#F0C808` (highlight bar, phone number).
- Cream `#F5F1E8` (headline and body text). Black `#0E0E0C` (text on gold).

## Layout
- Brand bar (green, gold top rule) at y 1400–1540: OHANA wordmark left, CALL OR TEXT + phone right, ending at x 940.
- Dark gradient behind the headline at the top and behind Instagram's caption at the bottom.
- End card: green panel, wordmark, READY WHEN **YOU** ARE, phone, service area. Text wordmark only until the real logo file is supplied.

## Motion
- Headline: fade + 40 px rise, 0.3 s ease-out, starting 0.1 s into the shot. Hold. Hard cut out.
- Photos: slow pan, max 160 px across the shot. No shaky zooms.
- One idea per shot.

## Sound
- Hit on frame one, whoosh on every cut, chime on the end card.
- Master to -14 LUFS, true peak under -1 dB. Music is added in the posting app, under the SFX.

## Content rules
- Real footage is captioned as Hansel or the Ohana crew. Marco Rojas (AI persona modeled on Hansel) appears only in generated scenes,
  and any post with a generated person carries the platform's AI label.
- Service claims limited to junk removal, clean-outs, hauling, property transitions.
- Blur plates, house numbers, names and the faces of minors before rendering.

## Export
- One MP4 plus `cover.jpg` (a frame with one big word on screen) per reel.
